Geography of Opinions

Organic, Glutton Free, AI Free

In October 2024 I developed a social network, organic, strictly without AI or any suggestion system. And here I want to describe the philosophy of it and relation to what I researched in AI.

I am puzzled, by all technology that we developed, and that still, we do not have a public platform, with public stakes, to organize ourselves politically and geographically. And after years of research in technology, the thought of a platform, that is AI-Free and organic to reflect political opinions of people geographically came to mind. Geography of Opinions, is a geographic social network, that is AI-Free, Suggestion-Free, for online voting, which gives control of how information flows through the network back to people.

Technology, is organization of our rational mind, as pixelation or digitization of perception, and in terms of action, is to pave the path between imagination and realization. But in the end, technology is described by finite number of letters, in any book. The scope of technology is the reduction of continuum of reality to finite set of of letters, in description, whether in a science book, or bits in a computer. And that is the clarity about its position in the wonders of “All and Everything". It is infinitely sumptous to think, that entirety of being can be summarized within finite letters. But there is a good use for technology, and that is to respond to our rational mind, that wants to predict and memorize and mechanize reality. Now in this perspective geoOpinions, responds to a part of our rational mind, human geographical organization.

We have something called an almost deterministic computer. Reality is stored in 0s or 1s, voltages and capacitors and transistors. And now, beside the stale talking point on definition of humanity for curiosity and futurism, we have a solid problem in our hands. We are 8 billion verbal beings, governed by theories of economics and politics. We have to ask the question, are these theories, that are in nature provide solutions for how numbers and votes are summed and divided, suitable for the complexity of the world today? I speculate that, what was proposed as technology of governance were at best, appropriate solutions, for a smaller population, and not for 8 billion people with smart phones and AI in their hand. with an unprecedented in scale, communicative and interactive complexity, I think its time to be serious and solve this once and for all: The Issue of Self Governance. And I acknowledge, that technology is not complete for self governance, but as I learnt to say, be in bed with your lover and play chess with Winston Churchill. Here I am going to talk about the Chess Part, the rational, and the white board and how to use the robot and epistemic thinking toward harmony. In this section, I will describe the current political system, while the complexity of the current systems is beyond my knowledge, but I will describe what is inside the scope of my expertise, the backbone schema of the decision making.


Intro

lets talk about USA as a test case and a thought experiment. Think about US senate. 100 senators sit in the chambers and decide on how to vote for 400 million and possibly affect the fate of the globe in voting. I have to ask everyone this question. Does those 100 senators, whoever they are at any time, and their advisors, and their polls, and their technology, complex enough to observe, the complexity of 400 million people and their pace of change? can they represent the will of public sufficiently? Simply, is the number of neurons in 100 multiplied by whatever the average number of neurons that exist in a senator’s brain, enough neurons, to represent the condition of 400 million of the average brain neurons of USA? whether the answer is true or false, there is no golden number called 100. maybe the optimal number is 400. maybe 1 million. Same goes with the number of years for a senator to be in senate, the interval between polls and voting. Does 4 years or 8 years or 20 years, or any of these numbers truly represent, the optimal time, that reflect average change of public attitude toward their decision making process? maybe we now change on monthly basis, and maybe we change on a yearly basis. 4 is not a universal constant. We simply do not know what is the optimal number. But as research in AI taught me, instead of finding the senator number for year 2026 or the measure of optimality, or the optimal interval, in a focus group, or a think tank, we can try to build a mechanism that is expressive enough to represent any complexity of the public. And now here is my proposal. Dynamic Democracy

Dynamic Democracy

‍ Starting from Polling. Polling has three components: the question, the vote and timing. To represent public digitally ( by digitally I mean in terms of verbal/numerical description) our model needs to allow public to propose the questions relevant to them, and to answer these questions freely. If the questions are proposed by the pollsters or in other words broadcasted, it should be admitted that the poll already includes bias by the pollster, And why not at any time? why not anytime public can change their vote and propose a new poll. This idea is not the new part of the proposal, direct and online democracy, but consider why we do not have this already. It is puzzling to me.

With public proposal of polls and their votes, we have a flat organization for polling and voting, each person can propose a poll, present choices and each can vote on the proposed question. Now lets talk about hierarchical structures.

Dynamic Democracy versus Static Representational Democracy:

In the example of US senate, 100 placeholder decision makers, are defined as representatives. This is the example of a hierarchy of decision making, with two layers. The public choose the top layer, of 100 representatives, and those 100 decide on what goes on in the senate.
Now Imagine, that for every propose question proposed by public, a citizen or an individual could delegate their vote to any other individual. The delegate has two votes on the question, and the delegate has the same choice, either vote directly or delegate the two votes to another. In this mechanism, In addition to the online aspect of the voting, where the votes can change at any time, the representation hierarchy can take any possible form in time. I call this Dynamic Democracy, vs the Static Representational Democracy. The structure of representation by this method of polling reflect another angle of expression of public in the political mechanism: the organization.

As an example, we can run a thought experiment on the effect of the representation organization. The legal language. Imagine that entirety of US tomorrow decide to be represented by people who only talk in Latin or Farsi. It is not far from imagination to predict, that the laws will be written in Farsi or Latin. This also goes with election of Lawyers, or Medical Doctors, or technologists. The public will have less understanding of the laws that are being passed. And I speculate that the deviation of legal language from the common english is also caused by having a strict one layer representational democracy. It should unease the public, that the laws that are imposed on them, is not understandable in their language by them. In the extreme case of direct democracy, Because each person votes directly on the polls, the polls that are popular, reflect also, the common language of the public. Another angle is voter turnout. By increased bias in the legislative system, and the deviation of the legal language from popular way of communication, I do not think, its sumptoues to link the separation of the legal system and its language from public condition, with lower voter turn out. As I can imagine, that if your votes to elect representative is independent of the outcome of their representation, the hesitation to vote is understandable.

I suspect that voter turn out, and the decline of the turn out globally, is reflective, of the incapability of the static representational democracy to catch up with accelerated increase, in population and complexity of the connectivity of society itself. Mathematicians and physicist have a trick in their pocket for proposing new theories, its called generalization. We say, a theory “A” is a generalization of theory “B”, if theory “B” is a special case of theory “A”. In this case, theory “A” is preferable to theory “B”, as theory “A” can explain theory “B” or what theory “B” explains, but the reverse is not necessarily true.
In our case, it is easy to show that Dynamic Democracy is a generalization of Static Representational Democracy. In the case of senate, in dynamic democracy, the public is able to delegate their vote, for all questions, to 100 senators, and change their vote once every 4 years, and except the delegates, nobody proposes questions.

Now what I am proposing is not a particularly complex, or out of reach of the mind concept, and that is why I am puzzled, that we don’t already have a platform since 2008, or government infrastructures to support this type of political system or a variation.

Neighbors and Geography

Geography and location of people on this sphere, is a determining factor about the trajectory of their interaction. One cannot, imagine the political reality in simply abstracted away in a server. The proposal is to ensure that public mobility and migration pattern is informed by the political reality, and that political reality is reflect in votes and opinions. As someone who has worked with complex dynamical systems, I cannot help but to advice that the political information be available on all scales to people, to help with their choice of home. And in a poetic sense, I want to advice on a fabric of neighbors, that neighbors are in harmony with their immediate neighbors the least, and not necessarily in harmony with a an abstracted away identity, or a manufactured political party. Toward this, in geography of opinions, alive beings, would pin their location on the globe, and with their proposed questions and their votes, they can see the dispersion of the votes across the globe. I propose a simple method for now, and that is how many of the questions you answered the same, as the similarity measure of opinions of a being to another. And visually I show this similarity by coloring the nodes on the map. No matter how this information, about opinions is made accessible to public, the objective remains the same, Informed migration and mobility. In addition to history of a place, in addition to the visual scenery, or the perceived culture, not only it would not hurt to have a clear view of the political and opinion variation or similarity of the place, but its a necessity for now, to calm the opinion conflict that lives within blocks of buildings or a city.

Each circle above, is a user, and the color of the circle represent the similarity of my opinions to them. This is the basic mechanism to show case the geographic aspect of the technological political theory. And in essence all political theories, becasuse of their descriptive and categorical nature, are technologic to begin with.
