Intro

Painting and words combine, to portray a scene, a whole. and I wished I could acompany both with the music that I listened to, when I painted each painting or wrote each letter. Audio, Visual, Verbal.

Neda Bostani, a beautiful spirit, a theoretical physicist and an auditory artist, which I met serendipitously in Reston Virgina, once advised me to maybe put a cheesepuff in my mouth to experience all the senses all at once when painting. But I have my tea and cigarettes, and I hope you have a bag of cheesepuff, or maybe tea around when reading this. And I hope you listen to a type of music that truly came from twists and turns of the sheet of the muscle of heart while reading, but none is a necessity. My suggestion is Maestro. Mohammad Reza Shajarian, and perhaps through the selection of his music curated by dear Soroush Kamalian.

and now a short description of where I walk.

In this world of numbers, additions and subtractions, and specially divisions; the writings and the paintings and the music, opened me to the mystique, and “Erfan”.

And Erfan in my eyes - this romantic, somewhat an eternal beg, for beauty and meaning, this mode of seeing and hearing and touching, and self dialogue, and prayer - can possibly translate to poetic mysticism. As the meaning departs, Aref (عارف), the witness of Erfan, pleas; with no exchange in mind, surrenders all their words, all their movement and all their will to the destiny of this garden, this admirable whirlwind of change. Admits the eventual surrender, not to a higher power, but to the ever unfold of meaning and beauty and pain and everything. And now, I cannot see any other sense in beauty, except through the lense of Erfan, that sense of wonder, sincerity and poetic beg, humility and tears that follow. Dear reader, I love tears, and in my mind, each drop is earned, and each drop is a diamond, and I never felt closer to The Garden, than the time that tears departed my eyes.

Some of the writings are segments of longer writings but they came mysteriously with minimal intentions, into this page. I hope you find coherency in skimming or reading through this page. and a quick note, these days I appreciate mistakes in grammar and spelling, a signature of alive speaking to alive, and apologies for the intended mistakes made by this unwise being.

The line, the shame, the war; letter to Eve


I am the one and the two and the three and the four

I am who, that drew these straight lines unlike those I drew before

I who imagined this and painted that

And when I looked close

And the straight lines curved

First time, I saw myself


Behind those lines, in the colors,

Behind those windows

There was your face

And you were in passing

And here I am, seeing you,

You who is familiar

The eyes known from a time behind time

In a place, behind space

In a home that was taken from us

Eden, this is what its called nowadays


And you were in passing

The winds of forgetting

Blending your image,

The wind, the drunken painter, the "mast",

And in the mixed colors I looked for you

And I begged the colors to unblend ,

And colors twisted and turned and I lost you even more, to hades, to red


And here I am

Sitting, waiting, worrying,

That I wont find you

The unspeakable

The impossible


But Eve,

Dear,

Whoever shame is, is shameless

In shame I wrote to you

In shame I saw you

And now, I write dearly to you

Who that used to live on the other side of the mirror

Who that is beautiful

Who that has no need for the fig leaves

Who that is the scene to be seen

And I am writing to you

To say, I remember now that I waited for you


And Eve, Theres the war,

Have you heard?

What can I say?

Happy family, we are?

Living forever, we are?

Remember when birds did not cry

Remember that ants did not bite

Remember, turtles used to fly

And I am ready to leave this pain behind

And I wished I could find more words

To Stop this war

This war, this shame, this war


Last thing

Dear eve,

I am truly yours and you are truly mine

And I will wait forever to have a glimpse of your smile

Behind that gold straight line
